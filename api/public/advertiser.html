What this is

Every click on your Botiny links is attested on a public chain (SKALE Europa testnet, gasless) by the Botiny attester, so you can inspect it without trusting our API: each click emits a Click event, and later status changes (held / vested / clawed back) emit StatusUpdate events. Nothing personal is published: the visitor is a daily-rotated salted hash, combined with the click id (never an IP).

Find your own events: the campaign salt

Each event carries a campaignHash (indexed topic). It is keccak256(utf8("botiny.campaign.v1|" + salt + "|" + advertiser_id + "|" + listing_id)). Your salt is private to you, so competitors cannot tell which events are yours. Compute the hash yourself (below it is computed in your browser) and filter eth_getLogs on the contract by topic 3 (Click) or topic 2 (StatusUpdate).
